- ingame/camera.rs::vehicle_camera: driver of a VehicleModel/ShipModel -> limit = camera_distance (wide in
  camera mode 1, normal otherwise); wanted snapped in mode 1, drawn distance eases with approach_distance;
  leaving restores the mode limit and clamps wanted (16 -> 10 as captured).
- hud/mode_actions.rs: the driven slave's slave_mount_skills replace the buff skills, in row order; the
  existing mode bar draws them (hotkeys from mode_action_bar_button: R T Y U, slot 5 unbound; cooldown and
  cast data from skills). A press sends CSStartSkill with the driven slave as the StartCaster::Mount (its object
  id and the slave_mount_skills row); the byte-exact test pins it. On SCUnitDetached the normal bar returns.
