Cart boarding (S5) - native 1.2 capture 2, 2026-10-28

How measured
- One hidden original 1.2 r208022 client through aaemu-launcher, driven only through
  aaemu-clientdriver (Lua, the client's own pointer/action/key calls; no OS input).
- Cart: slave template 60 (Farm Wagon), spawned with the server GM chat command
  /slave spawn 60. Positions come from ReadProcessMemory (PROCESS_VM_READ only).
- Pick: the pointer space is the 2554x1411 back buffer; X2Util:ConvertWorldToScreen does
  not agree with it on this client, so the probe sweeps right-click points around the
  projected cart and around the screen centre. The click that sends CSStartInteraction
  0x068 for the cart is the case click; the probe never clicks a second time from the
  state that click produced. Every attempt is a `sweep` row.
- Distance: /move to the cart + requested distance on the spawn axis, then up to three
  radial corrections (z is terrain height) until the measured 3D centre distance is
  within 0.08 m. The measured value is in each `placed` row.
- Server: the cap-live lane with the slave interaction fix (CSStartInteraction on a
  slave answers SCNpcInteractionSkillList with [12076]). RUST_LOG includes
  aaemu_game::net::fields=trace and aaemu_game::net=debug. The C->S field trace carries
  bodies; the S->C lines carry type, name, size and log time. S->C bodies are not in this
  trace level, so the comparison uses the sizes and the C->S fields.
- The client was started once for this table and stopped by pid. Two earlier launches
  produced no usable data (one aborted before capture on a probe bug, one invalidated
  because the first click already bound the rider and the probe clicked again); their
  output was discarded.

  C->S CSStartInteraction 0x068 body b4 73 00 | 00 00 00 | 01 00 00 00 | 00 00 00 00 |
       03 | 00 00 00 00  (npc cart, obj 0, extra 1, pick 0, mouse 3, mods 0)
  S->C SCNpcInteractionSkillList 0x07b len 34, log time 21:07:33 (one skill [12076])
  C->S CSBindSlave 0x031 body 4c 00 (u16 timeline 76)
  S->C SCUnitAttached 0x076 len 14, log time 21:07:33
  S->C SCSlaveBound 0x064 len 13, log time 21:07:33
  client: HasSlaveUnit true, mounted true, driver true; no SCErrorMsg 0x10a.
  samples: the player stands at the placed point until the bind, then the first 100 ms
  sample is already at the cart origin (one seat snap, no gradual walk).

(b) leave with X2SiegeWeapon:DiscardSiegeWeapon (standing)
  C->S CSDiscardSlave 0x032 body 4c 00; S->C SCUnitDetached 0x077 len 10; the client
  clears mounted/driver. No SCErrorMsg.

(c) reach
  3.5 m: 44 right-click sweep points, target empty and no C->S packet: the pick did not
  land from this slope/camera, so 0x068 was never sent. The same for 4.5 m (44 points).
  3.1 m (measured 3.102): 0x02c, 0x068, 0x07b len 34, CSBindSlave 0x031 4c 00 immediately
  (same second, no auto-walk), 0x076 len 14, 0x064 len 13. Player samples every 100 ms
  for 3 s: the only move is the seat snap at the first sample after the bind.
  2.9 m (measured 2.901): the same chain.
  No system message was shown and no SCErrorMsg arrived; 3.102 m is outside the port's
  3.0 m interaction reach but the client still sends 0x031.

(d) board, hold the client's own W for 2.6 s, leave while moving
  C->S CSMoveUnit 0x089 with the cart obj (47-byte driver body), ~10/s.
  GetSiegeWeaponSpeed 0.4; positions 0.3-0.5 m/s (the farm wagon is slow).
  DiscardSiegeWeapon 1.2 s into the drive: C->S CSDiscardSlave 0x032 4c 00 accepted,
  S->C SCUnitDetached 0x077 len 10; no 0x67, no SCErrorMsg. After the detach the held W
  moves the character on foot.

(e) right click a second time while bound
  Four of the 44 sweep points over the cart set the target: C->S CSChangeTarget 0x02c
  only. No 0x068, no 0x07b, no 0x031 and no visible message; the client does not start the
  interaction request from a seat on the same cart.

(f) a non-owner/passenger case needs a second player; skipped (one original client).

Differences from the code (behaviour not changed)
1. No auto-walk: at 3.102 m the native client sends CSBindSlave immediately after
   0x07b. The port refuses above 3.0 m with TOO_FAR_AWAY 0x9a. This agrees with the note
   that FUN_393bf270 skips the reach check when param_4 == 0.
2. While bound, the native right click sends only CSChangeTarget 0x02c; the port's
   slave::interact always sends 0x02c + 0x068.
3. At 3.5/4.5 m the native pick did not land at all in this capture; the port sends
   0x068 and then refuses with 0x9a. The capture cannot say whether a landing pick at
   those distances would bind; 3.1 m already binds, which makes a 3.5 m refusal unlikely.
4. The native cart drives at 0.4 m/s and DiscardSiegeWeapon is accepted while moving
   (no 0x67). The port's 5 m/s check uses the replicated movement velocity (unverified
   source), and cart driving is not ported.
