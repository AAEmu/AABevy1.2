Doodad affordances - results of the live capture of the original 1.2 client (2026-10-30)
=========================================================================================

Source: the lane's "doodads-12" capture (original archeage.exe r208022, hidden, driven through aaemu-clientdriver;
server trace of the same session): summary.txt, notes.txt, native.json, static.json, tools/dd_case_gather.py.
Screenshots stay local (never committed). Positions below are world coordinates; the driver's pose is the same
point minus a cell offset (multiples of 1024 per axis). The capture's database is the lane server's compact.sqlite3,
the one aa-client reads (`static.json` "db"), so func skills below are the same rows the client tables load.

NOT CAPTURED (stays capture-pending), one line each
---------------------------------------------------
* Use-target ring (4608/4610/4611/4612): the capture stood 5.5 m from 4608, outside its reach, and only took a
  screenshot; no radius, tint, or projected-versus-flat reading. In aa-client the ring follows the bar target, and
  the bar lists these four templates' FakeUse skill (the natural-gathering fallback), so it is drawn within 4 m.
* use_target_highlight colour and strength: screenshots only (tome 851, 1429 without highlight); nothing numeric, and
  1429 offered no action so no highlight state was compared.
* Hover plate (name / owner / growth timer): the 1.2 client exposes no tooltip text to the driver and the seedling
  offered no action; no plate string was read.
* use_target_silhouette: no 1.2 doodad row sets it; nothing to compare.
* Right-click walk-to on a doodad: not run for the ore (template 326 is absent from this database and GM-spawned
  doodads offer no action). summary.txt says an earlier far doodad right click only targeted, but native.json has
  no such row. The only recorded far right clicks were on a Farm Wagon (a slave unit): at 7.84 m and 27.76 m it
  targeted the wagon, sent no CSStartInteraction/CSBindSlave, and the player did not move for 6 s. That argues
  against walking, but it is a unit, not a doodad.
* The dynamic bar during the cast and after the use: only the cast bar was sampled, not the dynamic bar.
* Crate placement on the Farm Wagon: no crate positions were read.
