Doodad affordances (slice 4) - capture plan for the original 1.2 client
=======================================================================

STATUS (2026-10-30): the first live capture is summarised in capture-results.txt. It measured the bar reach (fixed in
code) and the tome / no-action readings of A, but not the ring (B: stood 5.5 m from 4608, outside the 4 m reach of its
fake-use skill 17557, screenshots only), the plate (C: no tooltip text), the phase message (D: 0x157 never sent) or the
doodad walk-to (E: not run). Those stay open below.

Owner: client lane. Everything here must come from the ORIGINAL client (archeage.exe, hidden) with a
packet capture, not from aa-client. Keep character names out of the images if the account is not a throwaway.
The client repository must not contain any of these images or the pak/db files; keep them in the lane's
`reference/` or `shots/` (git-ignored).

What is already implemented (docs/aa-client-doodads.md):
  * use_target_highlight  -> warm emissive tint (colour is ours)
  * use_target_decal      -> materials/decals/target/doodad.mtl ring, target_enemy04.dds (size/tint ours)
  * use_target_silhouette -> column mapped; 0 of 7124 doodad rows set it, no visual path
  * right-click walk-to   -> runs into reach, then uses (timing/stop distance unverified)
  * hover plate           -> name + owner (SCDoodadOriginator) + displayTime growth timer
  * SCDoodadPhaseMsg      -> DOODAD_PHASE_MSG(phase_msg) (packet never written by the lane server)

A. Use-target highlight (hover and focus)
------------------------------------------
1. Teleport to the Nuian start and stand 3 m from the Desireen Memory Tome (850, doodad_spawns
   15673.6,15176.2,121.0) and the Crescent Throne tome (851, 15379.9,13858.4,159.6).
2. Screenshots, same camera, 1920x1080 or the lane's usual size:
   - tome not hovered and not in reach,
   - tome hovered (pointer on it) but the character 5+ m away,
   - tome in reach (action bar visible) without hovering,
   - a use_target_highlight = false doodad (spawn 3446 "backpack storage box" on the farm wagon, or 1429
     "dirt mound") for the same three states.
   - the cart's storage crate (3446) close up: does the wood grain stay visible under the glow?
3. Record the colour of the glow (a DDS screenshot / pixel sample near the model edge) and whether it is a
   silhouette (visible through walls/other geometry) or a lit-model tint. If a second character can stand
   between camera and doodad, capture whether the doodad stays visible.
4. Packet capture: SCDoodadCreated for the tomes and the crate (template, phase, scale, owner), to time the
   glow to the action-bar state.

B. Use-target decal
-------------------
5. The only 1.2 templates with use_target_decal = true are 4608, 4610, 4611, 4612, the "space gap"
   continent portals (prefab portal.xml/continent.continent_portal_red). Find their spawns in
   doodad_spawns.json / GM /locate and go to one.
6. Screenshots: far, hovered, in reach; a low camera (eye level) and a top-down camera, so the ring's
   radius, ground projection (flat decal? follows terrain?), tint, texture (target_enemy04 ring?) and
   whether it also appears on the hover target can be read. Note if the native ring is a CryEngine
   projected decal (it may bend over terrain) or a flat card.
7. Capture the doodad object id and template of the ring target with the packet log; note whether the ring
   stays up during the cast and after the use.
8. Silhouette: try a quest doodad highlighted by a quest (quest_act_obj_* highlight_doodad_id) and the
   Kraken storm doodads (2852/2913/2928/2929/2993) which have use_target_highlight off - is any of them a
   silhouette? (No 1.2 column sets use_target_silhouette; this is a data/capture check only.)

C. Name + owner plate and growth timer
--------------------------------------
9. Plant a seed that creates an owned crop (item_spawn_doodads, e.g. a sapling/seed item) on a farm plot
   or house farm. Owner = the test character.
10. Hover the crop and screenshot the plate at T+5 s, T+60 s and just before the growth phase changes.
    Record the exact lines: name, owner line ("Owner: <name>"? localized key?), timer label (displayTime /
    timeLabel) and its format (MM:SS? "1h 2m"?). Take a second character's client and hover the crop: the
    owner line must still be the planter.
11. Packet capture: SCDoodadCreated (owner fields, plant_time, time_left), SCDoodadOriginator (owner
    change), SCDoodadPhaseChanged. Note whether the planted doodad is named by the item or the template.
12. Does the plate show without hovering (a permanent name tag over owned crops)? Capture a screenshot with
    the pointer away from the crop and another character walking past it.

D. DOODAD_PHASE_MSG bubble
--------------------------
13. Best targets: the arena countdown doodads (func groups 11127 to 11132: "The sword fight will begin
    soon..." and 5..1), the hungry elk calf (group 11019 "So hungry... Sniffle...") or the statue sayings
    (groups 1284 to 1286). Find spawns in doodad_spawns.json or quest instances.
14. Screenshot the moment of the message. Record where it appears: chat window, quest tracker/notify area,
    a 3D bubble over the doodad (GetDoodadScreenPosition / chatbubble.alb) or the center screen; capture
    the wording, colour and lifetime, and whether it repeats.
15. Packet capture with opcode 0x157 and its exact bytes: bc doodad object id + i32 message id. Confirm the
    second field is the doodad_func_groups.id by matching the fired text to that row's phase_msg
    (localized_texts en_us). Capture one is_msg_to_zone = false message (elk calf) and one zone message
    (arena countdown) while the doodad is out of the client's view / another sub-zone.
16. If the lane server ever writes the packet, capture the same two cases from it and compare bytes.

E. Walk-to
----------
17. Right-click a usable doodad 8 m away (Memory Tome, ore 326, sheep 518) and record:
    - whether the character walks at all (the earlier research says "out of reach: logged, nothing sent"),
    - the distance at which the use fires, the stop distance and whether it walks while the use is pending,
    - a key press during the walk, and whether the walk cancels,
    - timing: packet timestamps of CSStartSkill / the final movement packet relative to the click.
18. Repeat with an obstacle between the character and the doodad: does the native client path-find (click
    to move) or just run straight and get stuck? Note the timeout.

Deliverables
------------
* Packet capture (pcap or the lane's packet log) for one Memory Tome use, one planted crop life cycle,
  one decal portal and one phase message.
* Screenshots for A.2, B.6, C.10/C.12, D.14, E.17-E.18.
* A short text file listing the observed object ids/templates, the message strings and the plate format.
* Reference screenshots stay out of git (`reference/**/*.png` is ignored); JSON/Lua/packet dumps may be
  added to `reference/doodad-affordance/` with names, no game assets.
