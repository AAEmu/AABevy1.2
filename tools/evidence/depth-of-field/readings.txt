Depth of field: what 1.2 draws, and the frames beside ours.

1.2's frame, open land by day (D3D11 capture, r_driver DX10, sys_spec_full 4, 1920 x 1080):
  1. the lit HDR scene (R16G16B16A16_FLOAT) box-filtered to 960 x 540 (texToTexParams0/1 = +-a quarter texel: the four pixels
     of each block);
  2. the merged pass (motionblur.cfx, MotionBlurDof) blurs that copy into a second 960 x 540 target:
     vDofParamsFocus0 = (0.001, 0, -0.001, 0), vDofParamsFocus1 = (0, 0, 1, 0.479): range 2000, focus 0, 2b = 0.479;
     8 bilinear taps on a ring of min(4b, 2) texels (vLensSamples, one 960 x 540 texel each), the centre weighted 1/3;
  3. the composite (HDRToneMapSample, postprocess.cfx): lerp(scene, copy, saturate(saturate(z * x + y) + copy.a)),
     HDRDofParams = (2/r, -2d/r, d, 2b), all zero when 2b < 0.1.
  At rest that is a blend of 10 % of the copy at 100 m, 50 % at 500 m, all of it from 1000 m on.

The time of day at rest (TimeOfDayManager::UpdateEngine, cry3dengine+0xa5dd0): "Dof: focus range" (default 1000) and
"Dof: blur amount" (default 0); the effect takes focus 0, range 2 x the focus range, that blur. 114 of the 121 main world
zones set a blur at 12:30 (mean 0.17, most 0.30); Arcum Iris 0.237.

The quest talk (camera::set_user_dof, x2game+0x16a9e0): distance = camera_offset_y of the row, range 2.5 x that, blur 1;
the eight ghost rows (39 to 44, 50, 51) have no dof. The renderer eases its own copy of these values by min(3 dt, 1) every
frame, talk or not, from 3.5 m / 5 m / 1, and nothing resets them at the end of a talk: a talk's blur is full on its first frame.
Every row but the ghost rows also sets nv_dof, and on D3D11 the renderer then draws the talk on NVIDIA's Nv_Dof path, whose
kernel was not read. A native 1080p screenshot of a talk has the background's sharpest edges rising over 20 to 22 px
(10 to 90 %, linear light), which a gaussian of about 8 px standard deviation gives; that width is used here, as a fit.
