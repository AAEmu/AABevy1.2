Camera pivot evidence: the running 1.2 client against the fixed 1.754 m pivot and the measured pivot (2026-10-26)

native-ye-take1.csv.gz   tools/camera_probe.py against the 1.2 client (r208022), 4,419 samples in 167 s at 30 Hz. A Hariharan
                      woman standing on level ground at 19898 7513, camera mode level 1 (cl_fov 60, far limit 10 m). The sequence:
                      no input for 8 s after world entry, the wheel in one notch every 2 s to first person, out one notch every
                      2 s to the far limit, then a mouse drag down to the bottom stop and up to the top stop.
camera-run.txt        the same sequence as an aa-client script (zoom N / pitch DEG), for AA_CAMERA_LOG runs.

The two client runs compared below (not kept here: they are AA_CAMERA_LOG files of an aa-client build with the same orbit, run with
camera-run.txt at the same spot, classic mode, so their wheel went on to 18 m and the drag steps are the script's):
  fixed-pivot.csv     the orbit around a fixed pivot 1.754 m over the feet (this repository's camera before this change)
  measured-pivot.csv  the orbit around the camera bone's bind height + the .cdf lift (this change)
In those two logs target_above_feet is the pivot (T + lift); camera_log.rs in this change logs T itself, as the probe does, so in a
new log target and zoffset read the same as the native columns. Compare cam and look.

py -3 tools/camera_compare.py native-ye-take1.csv.gz fixed-pivot.csv measured-pivot.csv
